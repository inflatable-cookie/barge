Polish the remaining storage modules without changing what they promise: applying a move is now lock, revalidate, commit and rollback as separate steps, and a move plan reuses the binding identity, the schema constant and one source check instead of copies; the Parallels re-registration note is data (`tool_steps.rs`) the plan renders, not a special case in the move code. Releasing a hold waits only briefly for the area lock and skips its advisory size record, with the reason printed, instead of hanging, and a release and an eviction now record sizes through one shared checked helper. Root sizes are the space a root occupies (`st_blocks * 512`), so sparse and cloned VM disks no longer overstate a soft cap or freed bytes; a soft cap counts the area's fallback roots while the reserve counts only roots on its volume and credits only bytes freed there; and a reserve pass counts the evicted bytes and rechecks free space once at the end, so late APFS reporting cannot cause over-eviction. `Hold::into_inheritable`, `Hold::describe` and the unused `volume::Status` alias are removed.
