Close the storage-safety gaps from the quality read: a pin rechecks its root under the area lock, a waking hold checks the lock file it locked, adoption serialises the duplicate-ID check, and an unreadable pin counts as pinned. Deletion and measurement walk descriptor-relative from the folder whose ownership and device were read, so a replaced folder or mount cannot be walked. A size is approximate whenever an enumeration fails, and only a complete measurement is cached, so a stale or partial number is never reported as exact. Prune reports a failed rename as an error instead of a refusal.
